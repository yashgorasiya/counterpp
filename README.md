# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Counter** Android application ("App") developed by us.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, access, or store any personal information from users.

The App does not require registration or authentication and does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal data.

## Counter Data

Counter values and settings created while using the App are stored locally on your device.

We do not upload, transmit, or have access to this data.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose any user information to third parties because we do not collect any user information.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services to collect or process users' personal information.

## Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain personal user data on our servers.

Data stored locally on your device is protected according to the security mechanisms provided by your Android device.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults.

## Data Deletion

Because we do not collect or store user information on our servers, there is no personal information for us to delete.

You can delete locally stored App data through your Android device's App settings or by uninstalling the App.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of the App changes.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
